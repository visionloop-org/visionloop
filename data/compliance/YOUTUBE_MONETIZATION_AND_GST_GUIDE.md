## 🌍 1. Statutory GST on YouTube AdSense (Export of Services)

When you earn advertising revenue from YouTube/Google AdSense in India:

1. **Nature of Supply:** Classified as **Export of Services** (Place of Supply is outside India - Google Asia Pacific Pte. Ltd., Singapore / Google LLC, USA).
2. **Zero-Rated Tax Status:**
   * Under Section 16 of the IGST Act, 2017, export of services is **Zero-Rated (0% GST)**.
   * **Letter of Undertaking (LUT):** File a simple, free **LUT (Form GST RFD-11)** once a year on the GST portal (`gst.gov.in`) to export services without paying upfront 18% IGST.
3. **Foreign Inward Remittance Certificate (FIRC / e-FIRS):**
   * Whenever Google wires monthly AdSense earnings in USD/INR to your Vision Loop Current Account, the bank automatically issues a Foreign Inward Remittance Statement (FIRS) / e-BRC proving the foreign exchange inflow.

## 🇺🇸 2. US Tax Compliance (Form W-8BEN on Google AdSense)

To prevent the US Internal Revenue Service (IRS) from withholding a flat 30% tax on US viewer earnings:

* **Form Type:** Submit **Form W-8BEN** inside Google AdSense payment settings.
* **Beneficial Owner:** **Sapna Jaiswal** (Sole Proprietor of Vision Loop).
* **Tax Treaty Claim (Indo-US DTAA):**
  * Claim Article 12 (Royalties and Copyright) treaty benefits between India and the United States.
  * Reduces withholding tax rate on US viewership from **30% down to 15%**!
* **Tax Identification Number (Foreign TIN):** Enter your verified **Indian PAN Number** in the AdSense portal.
