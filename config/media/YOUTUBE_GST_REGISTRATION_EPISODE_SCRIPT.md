### ⚠️ Scene 5: Liabilities & Drawbacks of GST Registration — MUST WATCH (04:30 – 06:00)

* **Primary Visual:** Presenter on-camera with slower, serious tone. Red warning overlay cards. This is the most critical scene. Pacing is deliberate.
* **On-Screen Warning Cards (Red Border):**
  - ⚠️ **Monthly Filing Obligation:** GSTR-1 (due by 11th) + GSTR-3B (due by 20th) — mandatory every month, even if income is ₹0
  - ⚠️ **Late Filing Penalty:** ₹50/day for non-NIL returns | ₹20/day for NIL returns — up to ₹10,000 per return
  - ⚠️ **Interest on Tax Due:** 18% per annum on any unpaid GST liability from due date
  - ⚠️ **Annual Return (GSTR-9):** Mandatory if turnover exceeds ₹2 Crore. Audit (GSTR-9C) required if turnover exceeds ₹5 Crore
  - ⚠️ **Compliance Burden:** GST-compliant invoices, books of accounts, and records must be maintained for **6 years**
  - ⚠️ **B2C Disadvantage:** If your clients are individuals (not businesses), they cannot claim ITC — so adding 18% GST makes your services more expensive than an unregistered competitor
  - ⚠️ **Cancellation is NOT automatic:** Even if your turnover drops below the threshold later, you must actively apply for cancellation — or continue filing mandatory returns
